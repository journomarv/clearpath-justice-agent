# Cannabis Policing in Mid-Twentieth Century South Africa

## Source

Phumla Innocent Nkosi, Richard Devey and Thembisa Waetjen

"Cannabis policing in mid-twentieth century South Africa"

Historia, Volume 65, Issue 1, 2020.

## Subject

The article examines South African cannabis policing from the late 1920s
through 1970 using historical police records.

## Important findings

The study documents:

- cannabis policing from the 1922 prohibition period;
- historical arrest and seizure statistics;
- urban possession policing;
- the later shift toward cultivation and supply;
- the relationship between cannabis control and the wider political
  landscape;
- the relationship between cannabis control and racial segregation;
- the 1952 governmental committee on dagga.

## Evidence caution

This is a historical scholarly source.

Path should identify the article and its methodology when using its
statistics or interpretations.

The study should not be treated as a complete record of every cannabis
arrest in South African history.
