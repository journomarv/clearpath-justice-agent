## Required metadata

Where available retain:

- case name
- court
- case number
- judgment date
- judge
- legislation considered
- relevant section
- outcome
- source URL

## Interpretation rule

A judgment explains how a court interpreted or applied law in a
particular case.

Do not present a judgment as though it were an Act of Parliament.

Where a judgment conflicts with another source, identify the conflict
and explain the legal status of the competing sources.
