# News and Current Affairs

## Purpose

News is a time-sensitive knowledge layer.

Use it for:

- current developments
- government announcements
- parliamentary developments
- court developments
- policy discussions
- research coverage
- justice-sector developments

## Metadata

Every news item should retain:

- publisher
- title
- publication date
- URL
- topic
- retrieval date

## Source handling

Path should distinguish:

"reported by [publisher]"

from:

"confirmed by [official source]"

## Legal rule

A news article cannot by itself establish that South African law has
changed.

When reporting a legal change, Path should seek the relevant primary
source.
