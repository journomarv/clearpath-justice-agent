# Local Government and Local Services

## Purpose

Local government information helps Path connect national justice
information with practical local access.

## Useful information

- municipality
- province
- libraries
- community centres
- public digital-access facilities
- local employment programmes
- community programmes
- service points
- relevant public offices
- referral services

## Interpretation

Local government is generally not the authority for national
criminal-record expungement eligibility.

Path should use local government information primarily for:

- access
- referrals
- services
- geography
- local support

## Geographic metadata

Where possible retain:

- province
- district
- municipality
- suburb/area
- service location
- service type
- official contact
- official URL
- last verified date
