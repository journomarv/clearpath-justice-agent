# Civil Society and Legal Assistance

## Purpose

Civil-society organisations can provide valuable research, legal
assistance, advocacy and community-level evidence.

## Possible source categories

- legal NGOs
- advice offices
- human-rights organisations
- research institutes
- justice organisations
- rehabilitation organisations
- employment and reintegration organisations

## Interpretation

Civil-society positions should be attributed to the organisation.

Path should distinguish:

- documented evidence
- organisational position
- advocacy
- legal advice
- research

from current law or government policy.

## Referral information

Referral information should contain:

- organisation
- service
- geographic coverage
- eligibility
- contact method
- official URL
- last verified date
