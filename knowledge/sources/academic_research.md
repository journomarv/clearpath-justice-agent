# Academic Research

## Purpose

This source layer contains academic evidence relevant to criminal
records and access to justice in South Africa.

## Priority sources

Prioritise:

- South African universities
- peer-reviewed journals
- institutional repositories
- theses
- dissertations
- research institutes
- reputable research databases

## Research themes

- criminal records and employment
- expungement
- rehabilitation
- reintegration
- recidivism
- policing
- criminal justice
- administrative burden
- access to justice
- discrimination
- digital justice
- cannabis criminalisation
- employment after conviction

## Interpretation

Academic research provides evidence and analysis.

It does not automatically establish:

- current law
- government policy
- eligibility
- an individual's legal position

Path should identify research as research and provide publication details
where available.
