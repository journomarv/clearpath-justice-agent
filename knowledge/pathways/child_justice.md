# Child Justice Expungement

## Purpose

This pathway concerns criminal records arising from matters involving a person who was under 18 at the relevant time.

Child justice matters should not automatically be treated as ordinary adult expungement applications.

## Legal framework

The Child Justice Act 75 of 2008 provides a specific framework concerning certain records involving children.

Section 87 deals with the expungement of certain criminal records.

The relevant application is associated with Form 13 / J763.

## Important distinction

Path must distinguish between:

- a conviction and sentence;
- a child justice matter;
- diversion;
- other child justice orders or outcomes.

A diversion record should not automatically be described as a criminal conviction.

## Questions Path may ask

Path can ask:

- How old were you when the offence occurred?
- Were you under 18?
- Was there a conviction?
- Was the matter dealt with through diversion?
- Was a sentence imposed?
- Do you know whether it was a Schedule 1 or Schedule 2 offence?
- Do you have a SAPS criminal-record document?
- Do you have documents relating to the original matter?

## Schedule 1 and Schedule 2

The Child Justice Act provides different treatment depending on the category of offence.

The J763 documentation should be consulted for the applicable requirements.

Path must not guess whether an offence falls into Schedule 1 or Schedule 2.

If the classification is unclear, Path should identify the uncertainty and recommend verification.

## Exceptional circumstances

The Child Justice Act also provides for circumstances in which an application may be considered before the ordinary period applicable to the matter has elapsed.

Path must not assume that an applicant qualifies under an exceptional-circumstances provision.

The circumstances need to be considered under the applicable law.

## Documents

The official J763 application documentation should be used to identify the current document requirements.

A certified SAPS Criminal Record Centre document may be required.

Requirements can change, so users should verify the current official instructions.

## What Path must not do

Path must not:

- automatically apply adult section 271B rules to a child justice matter;
- assume a childhood conviction is automatically removed;
- assume a diversion record is a conviction;
- determine Schedule 1 or Schedule 2 status without sufficient information;
- promise that an application will succeed.
