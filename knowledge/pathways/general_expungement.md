# General Criminal Record Expungement

## Purpose

This pathway helps a person understand the general process for seeking removal or expungement of an adult criminal record in South Africa.

Path provides navigation and information only.

AI assists, not adjudicates.

Path must not tell a person that they definitely qualify for expungement unless the relevant legal authority has formally determined this.

## Legal framework

Section 271B of the Criminal Procedure Act 51 of 1977 provides a framework for the expungement of certain criminal records.

Eligibility depends on the circumstances of the conviction and sentence and the requirements applicable to the particular matter.

The official government guidance should be treated as the current starting point for the citizen-facing process.

## Information Path should ask for

Path may ask:

- Was the person an adult or under 18 when the matter occurred?
- Was there a conviction?
- Approximately when did the conviction occur?
- What sentence was imposed?
- Was the sentence completed?
- Was the sentence a fine, imprisonment, correctional supervision, suspended sentence or another form of sentence?
- Does the person have a current SAPS police-record document?
- Were there multiple convictions?
- Is the matter connected to a special statutory framework?

Path should avoid requesting unnecessary personal information.

Do not request:

- South African ID numbers
- full case numbers
- fingerprints
- passwords
- banking information
- unnecessary information about unrelated offences

## General process

A typical process may involve:

1. Establishing whether the matter may fall within an applicable expungement framework.
2. Obtaining the required SAPS criminal-record documentation.
3. Completing the relevant application documentation.
4. Gathering supporting documents.
5. Submitting the application through the current official channel.
6. Waiting for processing.
7. Receiving the outcome and any official documentation.

The exact submission method and administrative requirements can change.

Path must therefore encourage users to verify current requirements with the Department of Justice and Constitutional Development or the relevant authority.

## Timeframes

Official government guidance has referred to processing periods for expungement applications.

However, processing times can vary.

Path must not promise a specific completion date.

If a user asks:

"How long will my expungement take?"

Path should explain that published processing periods are indicative and that delays can occur.

## Important distinction

Expungement is not the same thing as obtaining a police clearance document.

A police clearance document provides information about criminal-record status.

It does not itself remove a criminal record.

## Path response principle

Path should explain the process in plain language and identify what information is missing.

It should not provide a definitive legal determination.
