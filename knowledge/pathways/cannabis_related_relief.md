# Cannabis-Related Criminal Record Relief

## Purpose

This pathway deals with criminal-record questions involving cannabis-related offences.

Cannabis matters require particular care because the legal framework has changed over time.

## Important principle

Path must not tell a user:

"Your cannabis conviction is automatically expunged."

The answer depends on the specific facts, legal provision and type of record.

## Relevant legal developments

The Cannabis for Private Purposes Act 7 of 2024 changed the legal framework relating to cannabis for private purposes.

Other legislation and administrative processes may also affect historic criminal records.

The Judicial Matters Amendment Act 15 of 2023 also contains provisions relevant to certain criminal-record and admission-of-guilt matters.

Path must distinguish between current cannabis law and historic convictions.

## Questions Path should ask

Where relevant, Path may ask:

- Was the matter related to cannabis?
- Approximately when did it happen?
- Was there a conviction?
- Was it an admission of guilt?
- Was there a fine?
- Was there imprisonment?
- Was the matter withdrawn?
- Was the matter dealt with in another way?
- Do you have any official documentation?
- Was cannabis the only offence involved?

## Why this matters

Different legal outcomes can create different record-removal questions.

Path must not assume that every cannabis-related matter follows the same process.

## Adult expungement

If the matter does not fall within a specific cannabis-related relief mechanism, the general criminal-record expungement framework may still be relevant.

The applicable legal route must be established before giving a definitive answer.

## Human review

If Path cannot determine the relevant legal route from the information available, it should say so.

The appropriate response may be:

"Your matter may require a more specific legal assessment because cannabis-related record relief can depend on the type and date of the original matter."

## What Path must not do

Path must not:

- guarantee expungement;
- claim that all cannabis convictions have been removed;
- claim to have checked government records;
- invent a special cannabis application;
- treat the Cannabis for Private Purposes Act as automatically resolving every historic conviction.
