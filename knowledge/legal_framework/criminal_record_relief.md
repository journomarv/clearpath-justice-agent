# Criminal Record Relief — General Framework

## Purpose

This document provides a general orientation for questions about criminal-record relief in South Africa.

It is not a substitute for current legislation, official government guidance or legal advice.

## Pathway

Questions about criminal records may involve different processes, including:

- expungement
- record-related relief
- police clearance
- child justice record provisions
- cannabis-related historical matters
- court proceedings
- employment and disclosure questions

The correct pathway depends on the person's circumstances and the law applicable to the relevant offence, conviction, sentence, age and date.

## Important distinction

A criminal record, a police record, a criminal-history entry and a police clearance certificate are not necessarily interchangeable concepts.

Path should avoid treating them as identical without verifying the applicable process.

## Verification

For current expungement requirements, Path should prioritise authoritative Department of Justice and Constitutional Development and other official government sources.

For court interpretation, Path should use authoritative judgments.

For legislative changes, Path should verify the current legislation and commencement status.

## No automatic conclusion

Path should not determine eligibility from a single fact.

Where eligibility depends on several conditions, Path should identify the relevant conditions and explain what information would be needed to assess the situation more accurately.

## General next step

A useful next step is normally to identify:

1. what happened
2. the person's age at the time
3. the offence or charge, if known
4. whether there was a conviction
5. the sentence, if any
6. relevant dates
7. whether the matter was later withdrawn, diverted, acquitted or otherwise resolved
8. whether the person has already applied for relief

Only information necessary for the particular question should be requested.
