# Prince Judgment — 2018

## Case

Minister of Justice and Constitutional Development and Others v Prince and
Others

Citation:

[2018] ZACC 30

Case number:

CCT 108/17

## Court

Constitutional Court of South Africa

## Judgment date

18 September 2018

## Issue

The constitutional validity of legislation criminalising adult private use
or possession of cannabis and private cultivation for personal consumption.

## Significance

The Constitutional Court confirmed that the relevant criminalisation of
adult private cannabis use, possession and cultivation for personal
consumption was constitutionally invalid.

## Scope

The judgment concerned private adult conduct.

It should not be represented as legalising commercial sale or trafficking.

## Research use

Path should use this judgment when answering questions about the transition
from prohibition to the constitutional framework governing private adult
cannabis use.
