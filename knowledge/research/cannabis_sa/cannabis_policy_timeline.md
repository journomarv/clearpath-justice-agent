# South African Cannabis Policy Timeline

| Period | Development | Evidence type |
|---|---|---|
| 1922 | Cannabis criminalised nationally as a dangerous drug | Historical/legal |
| 1928 | Medical, Dental and Pharmacy legislation consolidated controls over habit-forming drugs | Legislation |
| 1930s | Cannabis policing and eradication measures expanded | Police/government records |
| 1932 | Historical police records report 5,762 cannabis arrests | Police statistics |
| 1940s | Cannabis arrests increased substantially | Police statistics |
| 1952 | Committee on the Abuse of Dagga reported on cannabis control and policing | Government report |
| 1950s–60s | Policing increasingly targeted cultivation and supply while possession remained a major offence category | Police records/historical research |
| 1963/64 | Historical research reports 25,911 dagga prosecutions, approximately 90% for possession | Police statistics |
| 1971 | Abuse of Dependency-Producing Substances and Rehabilitation Centres Act introduced a more punitive drug-control framework | Legislation |
| 1994 onward | Constitutional rights framework changed the legal context for drug prohibition | Constitutional/legal |
| 2017 | Western Cape High Court declared aspects of cannabis prohibition unconstitutional | Court judgment |
| 2018 | Constitutional Court confirmed constitutional invalidity of criminalising adult private use, possession and private cultivation | Constitutional Court |
| 2024 | Cannabis for Private Purposes Act enacted | Legislation |


## Important qualification

This timeline is a research index, not a complete legislative history.

Each event must be linked to its underlying primary source before Path
presents it as definitive.
